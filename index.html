Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use HVAC Tracker ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle, collect, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Local Storage

HVAC Tracker is designed to function primarily as an offline tool. All of your personal and business data—including objects, clients, work logs, financial records, notes, and photos—is stored locally on your device. The Developer does not have access to your local database, does not collect this information, and does not transmit it to any third-party servers.

2. Google Drive Sync and Cloud Storage

To provide cross-device synchronization and secure backups, the App offers an optional cloud sync feature using your personal Google Drive account.

If you choose to enable this feature, the App will request access to your Google Account. HVAC Tracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Restricted Scope: The App requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This grants the App access only to a hidden, application-specific folder in your Google Drive. The App cannot see, read, modify, or delete any of your personal files or documents stored elsewhere in your Google Drive.
  • Data Ownership: Your synced data remains entirely under your control and counts against your personal Google Drive storage quota. The Developer has zero access to your Google Drive account or the encrypted backups stored within it.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App does not use any third-party analytics trackers, crash reporting tools (such as Firebase Crashlytics), or advertising networks. We do not track your usage behavior or monetize your data in any way.

4. App Permissions

The App may request the following permissions on your device to function correctly:

  • Camera and Storage: To attach photos and documents to your work logs.
  • Notifications: To remind you of upcoming deadlines and daily work logs.
  • Biometrics: To secure the App with your fingerprint or face scan (this biometric data never leaves your device's secure hardware).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.
